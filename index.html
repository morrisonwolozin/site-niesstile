An experienced craftsman

Jeff Niess learned the trade in Eastern Pennsylvania before moving to Maine six years ago. Before deciding to specialize in tile setting, Jeff worked as a home building and renovation generalist. The culmination of these learned skills leads to a deep understanding of the process, proper execution, and a polished finished project for every customer.

Niess Tile works with homeowners and general contractors to install tile in kitchens, bathrooms, and anywhere else in the home. Niess Tile is a trusted trade partner of Northridge Construction LLC and performs standalone renovation and new construction projects directly with homeowners across Central and Midcoast Maine.
