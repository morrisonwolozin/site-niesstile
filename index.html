Common Questions

What areas do you serve? +

Central and Midcoast Maine. Greater Bangor and Brewer are typical locations. Also Belfast, Lincolnville and Camden.

Do you supply the tile, or do I? +

We'll do it either way, but the customer will select the tile. Twin City Tile in Brewer, ME has a nice showroom with many choices.

How far in advance should I book? +

Two months is a typical lead time for most projects.

Do you work directly with homeowners, or only through contractors? +

Most often, Niess Tile works directly with homeowners. Also, Niess Tile is a trusted trade partner of Northridge Construction LLC and other general contractors.

How much extra tile should I buy? Do I need to account for cuts and waste? +

I will help you determine the amount of tile needed for your project, accounting for cuts and waste. Also, it's always a good idea to purchase a little extra in case of breakage or future repairs.
